How to Play Frostveil

Master the algorithms of containment to protect the realm.

1. The Realm & The Citadel

Each node is a settlement with population 1–9. One settlement is the Citadel (♛). If frost ever reaches the Citadel, the realm falls immediately!

2. Warden Phase Actions

Each turn you have k actions (1 or 2). Spend each action to either:
• Light Beacon (🔥): Click a CLEAR settlement to make it WARDED (blocks frost forever).
• Sever Road (✂️): Click a road edge to demolish it so frost cannot cross.
Use Undo (U) to revert actions within the current turn.

3. Frost Spread & Containment

When you click End Turn (E), every FROSTED (❄️) node spreads frost across remaining roads to all adjacent CLEAR nodes (one BFS layer). The game ends when no frosted node has a clear neighbor.

4. Overlays, Hints & Stars

• Forecast (F): BFS arrival turns on each node (`✓` = unreachable).
• Safe Zones (Z): DFS connected clear regions & total population.
• Critical (C): Tarjan articulation points & bridge roads.
• Stars: ★ Win · ★★ Match Articulation-Aware Greedy · ★★★ ≥95% of Best Known / Optimal.
