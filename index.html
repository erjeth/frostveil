How to Play Frostveil

1. The Realm & The Citadel

Each node is a settlement with population 1–9. One settlement is the Citadel (♛). If frost ever reaches the Citadel, the realm falls immediately.

2. Warden Phase Actions

Each turn you have k actions (1 or 2). Spend each action to either:
• Light Beacon: Click a CLEAR settlement to make it WARDED (blocks frost forever).
• Sever Road: Click a road edge to demolish it so frost cannot cross.

3. Frost Spread & Containment

When you click End Turn (E), every FROSTED node spreads frost across remaining roads to all adjacent CLEAR nodes (one BFS layer). Containment occurs when no frosted node has a clear neighbor.

4. Tactical Lenses & Stars

• Forecast (F): BFS arrival turns (`✓` = unreachable).
• Safe Zones (Z): DFS connected clear regions & population.
• Critical (C): Tarjan articulation points & bridges.
• Stars: ★ Win · ★★ Match Greedy · ★★★ ≥95% Optimal.
